Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026 | Applies to: Shanlu

Wang Jieyu (trade name: Meikyo Studio, "we" / "us"), as the operator of the self-reflection journaling and habit-tracking app "Shanlu" (the "App"), takes the protection of your personal information seriously and complies with applicable laws including the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (Japan) and the GDPR (EU).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Email address (for account registration and login)
  • Nickname (optional, shown in-app only)
  • Reflection journal entries, check-in records, habit records
  • Date of birth (optional) — used only for the optional "Eastern personality lens" self-understanding feature; never used to predict the future
  • Contact details (only when you use customer support)

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • App usage logs (crash reports, performance data)
  • Anonymized statistical data

1.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • Precise location, contacts, photos, microphone (without explicit permission)
  • Credit card numbers (handled by Apple App Store on iOS)
  • Government ID numbers
  • Advertising tracking identifiers (we do not perform ad tracking)

2. How We Use Information

  • Generating daily reflection prompts, weekly reviews, and AI companion notes
  • Providing core features such as habit records and growth visualization
  • Improving the product and fixing bugs
  • Customer support and compliance with legal obligations
We never use your personal information for ad targeting, and we never sell it to third parties.

3. AI Processing (Important)

Features such as "AI companion" and "insight summaries" send your user content (e.g., reflection text) to an external AI service (DeepSeek API) over encrypted communication to generate responses.

  • The data is used only to generate responses; we remove directly identifying information (such as your name) where possible (anonymization).
  • AI-generated content is informational only and does not replace professional advice (medical, psychological, legal, financial).
  • If you do not wish to use AI features, you can avoid this transmission by not using them.

5. Data Storage

  • Records and check-in data are stored primarily on your device.
  • Only if you enable account sync are they uploaded, encrypted, to servers we manage.
  • Server retention: during the life of your account + complete deletion within 30 days after account closure.

6. Your Rights

  • Access & export your data (in-app "My Page → Export", JSON / Markdown)
  • Rectify your information
  • Delete your account and all data (available in-app; full deletion within 30 days)
  • Withdraw consent (turn off sync / stop using AI features)
  • Data portability

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have GDPR rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and objection. Contact us below to exercise them.

7. Minors

The App is not directed to children under 13. In the EU/EEA, users under 16 should use it with the consent of a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via in-app notice or email.
